# Hellgate (GDLM 44): the benchmark level

Our first uploaded level (the phone slot). Evgeni, 2026-11-28: "Let's say there's a benchmark, as it's our first uploaded
level is Hellgate. Eventually I want it to be the full length of the song, maybe with the last minute just showing some of
the things it can do besides the raw gameplay, like maybe 40 seconds of some cool auto. First with gameplay we see, later
with some more weird stuff, and then in the end maybe 20 seconds in the end of the song we just remove the gameplay
completely and do some cool animation to the beat of the song and to the ending."

So every new pipeline (section generators, feel, deco, auto, effects) is measured on Hellgate first. A rebuild uses the
newest methods, never patches an old version (BUILDS.md has every version's retro).

## The target: the whole song (custom 343707, 231 s)

Cut at the song's own transitions (`node tools/sections.js 343707`), so each part starts and ends where the music changes:

| song time | part | what | tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 - 176.1 s | gameplay | sections generated by type, feel per section, the hellgate design theme with a sky and backdrop per section; starts on its first beat, up on blocks | tools/typebuild.js (briefs/44_hellgate.types.json) |
| 176.1 - 190.2 s | auto, gameplay you can see | no clicks, but it still looks like play: pads, orbs and portals take the player through structures on the beat | tools/autobeat.js, tools/autobricks.js, tools/autoclose.js (AUTO.md) |
| 190.2 - 215.4 s | auto, weirder | what raw gameplay can't do: camera turns and zoom, rotated gameplay, speed bursts, dual, moving structures, teleports | tools/effects.js (EFFECTS.md), AUTO.md, the rotated / moving-object physics |
| 215.4 - 231 s | no gameplay | "the gate closes": an animation to the beat and the song's ending, the doors slam over the player on the last hit, the quiet sign in its last 5 s | **tools/hellgate_ending.js** (built, standalone: see below), tools/effects.js, tools/signature.js |

About 39 s of auto and 16 s of animation, which is Evgeni's "~40 s" and "~20 s" placed on the song's cuts. Calm effects
only, no pulses or flashy stuff (his rule since 2026-11-27), except where the animation part is the show itself: check with
him before that part goes in.

## Order of work

Evgeni's focus rule: the section generators come first. So the order is:

1. The gameplay part, grown section by section as the generators improve (now: to the first drop, 52.8 s).
2. The gameplay to 176 s, once the generators hold up over a long level (variety across 15+ sections, no repeats).
3. The two auto parts, from the auto toolkit as it is then.
4. The animation ending.

Each step goes to the viewer first, then gets the judge report. Nothing is installed or uploaded without his OK; the phone
slot keeps the uploaded version until he says to update it.

## Where it stands

- **v13 (2026-11-27):** the first build by the section-type pipeline. 1.1 - 52.8 s, 7 sections, the drop a 2x ship. Judge 4.1/10
  (transitions 1, variety 0, ending 4), Normal ~3 attempts, tightest press 142 ms. Open in the shared builder (the GDLM 76
  thread): portals walked into, the upside-down section built upright, ~9 s of nothing before the sign.
- The uploaded version on the phone slot is older (PUBLISH.md, local/phone_ids.json).

- **The ending (2026-11-28): "the gate closes"**, Evgeni's pick of four ideas (the others: a recap of the level's own
  structures and route, a descent through layers of rock, a forge sigil built on the strong notes -- kept here for later).
  `node tools/hellgate_ending.js` -> `levels/gdlm_44_hellgate_ending.gdlvl` ("GDLM 44 hellgate ending"), a standalone auto
  level that starts at song 213.8 s (kA13) and ends after the song (232.5 s); `build()` works in song seconds and `parts(ctx,
  songT)` returns the show alone, so it can be stitched after the auto part. Timeline on the measured notes: lead-in dark ->
  pillars rise (215.4 / 216.3) and their fire caps light one a beat -> 11 arch stones fly in and land on the beats
  (218.7-220.4, keystone 221.2) -> the loudest bars (222.0-227.0): a ring of 8 runes turns 1.5 times, one rune lit per beat,
  caps flare on the strongest notes, the sky warms to red -> the music thins: the ring slows, runes dim -> 228.25 (the last
  big hit): the doors slam shut over the player, a hot seam, one sky flash, the stage thuds -> the gate dims into black, the
  quiet sign. Move / rotate / alpha / colour only, all locked to the player's x (move 58) so the stage stays on the screen;
  603 objects, 351 triggers, 61 groups. Test: `node tests/hellgate_ending.js`.
  Not checked in GD yet: the doors are on T1, which should draw over the player in the game (the viewer always draws the
  player on top); a rotate about a centre that is itself locked to the player; the quiet sign's camera lock (the viewer
  draws the sign unlocked, so it drifts left over the right tower there -- in GD it should stay put right of the tower).
